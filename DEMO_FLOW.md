# Site Office demo walkthrough

The laptop Android window and http://localhost:8081 use the device demo, existing sample records and live Gemini. Company sharing and external delivery require cloud activation first.

1. Start on Home: show recorded deadlines and pending priorities.
2. Use the microphone for a short site briefing. Stop, review/edit the transcript and use the request. Inspect the answer's sources.
3. Open the bill checker: compare 100 cement bags invoiced with 90 received at Rs. 2,300 each. Review the Rs. 23,000 delivery variance.
4. Request a follow-up task, edit its proposed details, approve it and find it in the task workflow. Local approvals produce local demo work.
5. Explore all fourteen specialists under Agents. Additional local forms explicitly identify themselves as device demo drafts.
6. Show preserved Activity and Profile, including motion preferences and Company workspace.

After following CLOUD_SETUP.md, demonstrate the authenticated company workflows: owner MFA and a supervisor restricted to one site; private uploads and cited agent proposals; exact-revision combined approvals; staging Gmail/Drive; estimates, attendance corrections and payroll; payment instructions and recorded bank references; per-site costs and audit investigations; permits and reports; offline capture/restart/conflict recovery; background work with the app closed; and owner health/usage views.

Use an explicitly chosen staging mailbox for any real sending. The app prepares payment instructions and tracks recorded payments; it does not move money. Validate Sinhala with a speaker. Physical Android, real cloud sharing, push, signed installation and live backup restoration remain separate pilot checks. iOS currently has bundle compilation evidence only.

The extended voice/text rehearsal and the latest bug audit are in DEMO_AUDIT.md. Specialist records are now available to Chat on this device; prompt actions can create or update records across the specialist workflows after review. Background delivery still needs the connected company services.
